Frequently asked questions

Before you get started

How do I book an assessment?

Call or email Anton directly. Briefly describe your goals and any relevant training or movement history, and you can agree on a suitable location and time.

Which location should I choose?

The best location depends on the coaching service, your goals and practical travel preference. Contact Anton if you would like help choosing.

Can I train while working with a physiotherapist?

Potentially, yes. Exercise coaching can be adjusted around existing medical or physiotherapy recommendations and does not replace clinical treatment.

Do you coach athletes?

Yes. Coaching can support strength athletes and combat-sport athletes, with physical preparation adjusted around technical training and competition demands.

Do I need previous training experience?

No. The starting point is based on your current capacity and goals, whether you are new to structured training or already experienced.
