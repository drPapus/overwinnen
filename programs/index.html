Medical Fitness

Medical Fitness

Individual exercise coaching designed to improve movement quality, physical capacity and confidence when returning to training or managing recurring physical limitations.

  • Individual movement assessment
  • Mobility and stability development
  • Progressive strength training
  • Load management
  • Return to activity
  • Coordination with medical guidance when required

Structured strength coaching for people who want to become stronger, improve lifting technique and build long-term physical capacity.

  • Competition preparation

03

Athlete Preparation

Combat Sports Performance

Strength and conditioning for MMA, boxing, kickboxing, wrestling and grappling athletes who need stronger, more resilient and better-conditioned bodies.

  • General physical preparation
  • Maximum and explosive strength
  • Strength endurance
  • Energy system development
  • Neck, trunk and grip preparation
  • Load management around technical training
  • Competition preparation

Preparation is adjusted to sport demands, schedule, competition calendar, athlete level, injury history and current capacity.

AIM Method integration

Different goals. One adaptive process.

  1. 01
    Assessment

    Understand movement, history, capacity and goals.

  2. 02
    Adaptation

    Select exercises, volume, intensity and complexity according to the individual starting point.

  3. 03
    Performance

    Develop the physical qualities required for daily life, strength training or sport.

  4. 04
    Recovery

    Manage fatigue and maintain sustainable long-term progression.

The programme changes according to the person. The method remains consistent.

Individual by design

No programme should exist without context

Generic programming

  • Fixed exercise selection
  • Fixed progression
  • Little consideration of training history
  • No integration with technical sport training
  • Intensity without adequate feedback

Adaptive Motion Intelligence

AIM programming

  • Individual assessment
  • Goal-specific exercise selection
  • Load matched to current capacity
  • Ongoing observation and adjustment
  • Recovery integrated into the plan
  • Coordination with sport or medical guidance when needed
