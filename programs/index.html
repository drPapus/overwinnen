AIM Method integration

Different goals. One adaptive process.

  1. 01
    Assessment

    Understand movement, history, capacity and goals.

  2. 02
    Adaptation

    Select exercises, volume, intensity and complexity according to the individual starting point.

  3. 03
    Performance

    Develop the physical qualities required for daily life, strength training or sport.

  4. 04
    Recovery

    Manage fatigue and maintain sustainable long-term progression.

The programme changes according to the person. The method remains consistent.

Individual by design

No programme should exist without context

Generic programming

  • Fixed exercise selection
  • Fixed progression
  • Little consideration of training history
  • No integration with technical sport training
  • Intensity without adequate feedback

Adaptive Motion Intelligence

AIM programming

  • Individual assessment
  • Goal-specific exercise selection
  • Load matched to current capacity
  • Ongoing observation and adjustment
  • Recovery integrated into the plan
  • Coordination with sport or medical guidance when needed
